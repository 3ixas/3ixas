# Elias Bennett

I’m a software engineer based in London. At BNP Paribas, I build pricing and risk engines in C# and Java. Outside work, I build products of my own with TypeScript and Python.

I like taking an idea the whole way through: understanding the problem, designing the interaction, building it, and checking whether it works.

## Things I make

- **[Threshold](https://github.com/3ixas/threshold)** — A rental affordability calculator for London, Basel and Zurich. Built to make the costs of moving easier to understand and compare.
- **[Argus Risk](https://github.com/3ixas/argus-risk)** — An event-driven portfolio risk simulator using .NET, Kafka, PostgreSQL, SignalR and Next.js.
- **[Flowtime](https://github.com/3ixas/flowtime-focus-timer)** — A focus timer with breaks based on how long you actually worked, offline support and session recovery.
- **[eliasb.dev](https://github.com/3ixas/eliasb.dev)** — My personal site: the work, how I build it, and what I do off the clock.
